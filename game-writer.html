About

Story-first writing with player clarity in mind.

I am a Game Writer and Narrative Designer in transition from technical support leadership into game development, with a focus on interactive storytelling, branching structure, and player-centered communication. My background has shaped the way I think about clarity, empathy, feedback, and how writing can guide players through systems without losing narrative tone.

Writing focus

Core strengths.

  • Dialogue and scene writing that reflects motive, conflict, and tone.
  • Interactive writing that treats choice as part of story design, not just branching for its own sake.
  • Attention to structure, pacing, usability, and the relationship between story and gameplay flow.
  • Dialogue systems, branching scenes, and character-driven choices.
  • Lore, codex-style writing, and in-world narrative framing.
  • Narrative breakdowns that connect writing decisions to player experience.
